Rainarr privacy policy

Effective September 23, 2026 · Version française (the French version prevails)

In short

  • Rainarr is a mobile app that controls your own media servers (Plex, Radarr, Sonarr, Prowlarr, Tautulli, Seerr, torrent client).
  • Rainarr has no server: no data ever reaches the publisher.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no crash reports sent.
  • Your credentials stay on your device, encrypted.

Who publishes Rainarr

Rainarr is published by Rick Deckard, an individual residing in France. For any question about your data: card.dicked@gmail.com.

The publisher does not receive, store or process any personal data. The processing described below happens on your device, at your initiative, or at the services you choose to connect.

What is stored on your device

Data Protection
Your server addresses, API keys, tokens, and the credentials of your torrent client and sources Encrypted, with a key held by the system (Android Keystore, iOS Keychain)
If you join a server as a guest: the Seerr server address, session, display name (your email address if your account has no name) and avatar Encrypted
Search history (last 8 movie and series searches) Encrypted
Favorites, tracked downloads, home screen layout, chosen language App-private storage
Cached posters and avatars App-private storage, renewed automatically

If you scan an invitation QR code, the camera is only requested at that moment. The image, from the camera or your gallery, is read on your device and then discarded: it is neither saved nor sent, only the server address is kept.

This data does not leave your device through a backup: Rainarr is excluded from Google backup and phone-to-phone transfer, and its encryption key does not follow an iCloud backup.

What is sent, and to whom

To your servers. Rainarr sends each server you configure what it needs to control it: its key or your credentials, your searches, and the movies and series you add, request or delete. You choose these servers; the publisher has no access to them. Credentials are only sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), or to your local network.

In the background, if you turn on notifications: about every fifteen minutes, Rainarr asks those same servers what changed — your requests, new requests, finished downloads, services that stopped responding. Nothing else is contacted, and it all stays off until you turn on a notification.

If you report a problem: Rainarr prepares a report — version, phone model, language and recent errors, with no address or credential — and shows it to you in full. It only leaves if you share it, and only to where you choose to send it.

To a few third-party services, which also receive your device's IP address:

Service Data Why
TMDB (The Movie Database) Your searches, the titles you open, the app language and your device's country Show the catalog, details and posters
Plex (plex.tv) An identifier specific to your installation; signing in to your Plex account happens on Plex's website, in your browser Sign you in as a guest to a server; show the accounts you share your server with
YouTube The trailer's identifier Show its thumbnail and open it when you tap it
Indexers you configure Your searches; then fetching the torrent file from the address the indexer gives, which may be another site Find a download and send it to your client

Each of these services processes this data under its own policy.

If you join a server as a guest

You sign in with your Plex account, on Plex's website. Rainarr then receives a token for your account, passes it to the Seerr server you are joining to open your session, and does not keep it. If that server allows new Plex users to sign in, this first sign-in creates an account in your name there, based on your Plex account; otherwise it is refused.

That account belongs to the Seerr server, not to Rainarr. When you leave a server, Rainarr closes your session and erases what it kept about it on your device, but your account stays on the server: only the person who runs it can delete it, and you should ask them to.

How long, and how to erase

  • Your data stays on your device until you erase it.
  • Settings → Erase all data removes everything Rainarr keeps on the device and closes your guest sessions. Nothing is deleted on your servers.
  • Uninstalling the app also erases its data.

Your rights

As the publisher holds no data about you, your rights of access, rectification and erasure are exercised directly in the app. For data held by your servers or by third-party services, contact their administrator or publisher. For any question: card.dicked@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority.

Minors

Rainarr is intended for people aged 18 and over.
